OTP ATL · listing review

Bare rooms and a deep move-in discount invite short stays

From the public PadSplit listing and its 35 photos, checked against the house's own numbers.
What the listing showsWhat the numbers showFix
The host approves 27% of booking requests and takes about 11 hours to respond.51 booking requests never became move-ins, and 8 of the 21 who did move in left within 30 days.Respond to every request within 1–2 hours, not 11: the best applicants book elsewhere while they wait. And raise the quality of who applies with a stronger listing: furnished rooms, more photos, clear house rules and no deep move-in discount, so fewer requests need turning down.
Each room gets about two photos: a full bed, one small nightstand and wall art. No closet, dresser, desk, chair or TV.New members leave in the first month: 8 of 21 this year.Add the furniture members live with: a desk and chair, a TV, and storage (a dresser or a clothing rack). Then show it: 4–5 photos per room, including the closet or storage, so members know where their things go. List what each room includes in its description. About $200 a room ($2,000 for the house) could be worth about $250 a month in higher rents and fewer early exits (illustration on the next page).
Move-in promo: 30% off the first 6 weeks (live listing, November).Earlier promos ran about 10%, and 8 of 21 new members already left in the first month.A deep first-weeks discount rewards exactly the member who leaves when it ends. Drop the promo and, if rooms need help filling, lower the base price a little instead: every member gets it, for as long as they stay.
Wide-angle photos bend the walls and make rooms look warped.Most rooms are occupied today.Straighten the existing photos with lens correction now. Reshoot each room at its next turnover, once it's furnished; until then, virtual staging (free with ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude) can show the new furniture.
No public reviews yet ("First reviews coming soon").Members who stay long, like Rooms 4 and 5 with no move-outs all year, are the ones to ask.Offer a small gift card for leaving a move-in review. Reviews are the first thing a member checks.
Stay Rewards: 1 week free after 6 months.Settled members stay; the losses are in month one.Keep it. It rewards exactly the members this house needs.
What this means: The house loses members in the first month, and the listing sets that up: rooms that look like a bed and nothing else, and a 30% discount that ends at week 6. Paired with a 27% approval rate, the house turns away most people who ask and still loses more than a third of the ones it accepts before day 30. Furnish and photograph the rooms as places to live, and price for members who stay.
